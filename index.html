About Me

Hi, I'm Ruolin Zhang, currently a research assistant at the MC² Lab at HKUST (Guangzhou).

My research interests center on AI agents in extended reality, particularly their potential for education and creativity tools. I am interested in how intelligent, embodied systems can support learning, expression, and meaningful human experiences. I am also interested in wearable computing, especially technologies designed to support physical and mental well-being. CV

Before transitioning to HCI research, I worked as a director and screenwriter (Film Portfolio).

Outside research, I enjoy rock climbing 🧗‍♀️ and ballet 🩰. I studied the erhu for eight years and the violin for two years 🎻, and I love listening to classical music.

Research

The Convergence of Minds and Bodies: A Systematic Review of Generative AI Agents in Extended Reality for Education

Ruolin Zhang, Zheng Wei, Pan Hui

Under review at TVCG.

↗ Preprint PDF

A PRISMA-based systematic review of 73 studies, synthesizing the roles of generative AI agents in XR education and deriving design implications for future learning experiences.

Visiting Alone, but Not Alone: Exploring AI Guides and Peers in Solo VR Museum Visits

Anonymous Research Team

Under review at CHI 2027.

↗ Preprint PDF

A comparative study of five AI agent configurations in a VR museum, using a within-participant design with 27 participants to examine how guide and peer roles shape companionship, learning, and interaction demands during solo visits.
